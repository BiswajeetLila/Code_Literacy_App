## Player Instructions

Give the player only what the final game gives them.

## Observe

- Goal clarity.
- Control clarity.
- Feedback clarity.
- Difficulty spikes.
- Confusion points.
- Bugs.
- Moments of fun.

## Questions

- What did you think the goal was?
- What was confusing?
- What felt good?
- Where did you want to stop?
- What would you change first?
